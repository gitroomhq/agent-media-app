Margin analysis — do we have 70%?

Short answer: No on video skills (~32–48%), yes on image skills (~70–95%). Math below.

The two rates

  • Revenue (what users pay): $100 → 6,800 credits = 68 credits per $1 = $0.01471 per credit. (Smaller $10 tier is 65 cr/$ — slightly better for us; using the standard 68 cr/$ which most buyers get.)
  • Cost (EvoLink Seedance 2.0, 720p — what we call): $0.199 / second → 5s $0.995, 10s $1.99, 15s $2.99. (Confirmed: our test reserved 135 EvoLink credits for 10s = $1.99.)
  • Cost (OpenAI gpt-image-2, per image): ~$0.04–0.17 — estimated, exact gpt-image-2 price not pulled. Using ~$0.10 mid.

Why the videos miss 70%

Seedance 720p costs $0.199/sec. At 200 credits for a 10s clip we collect $2.94 against a $1.99 cost — only 32% margin. The credit price is basically pinned to EvoLink's own credit price (both ≈ $0.0147/credit), so we're reselling Seedance at ~1.5× cost, not 3.3×.

What 70% margin would require

For 70% margin, cost must be ≤30% of revenue → revenue ≥ cost ÷ 0.30:

Levers to hit 70%

  • Raise video credit cost ~2.25× (10s selfie/lip_sync 200 → ~450 cr; ugc 285 → ~500 cr). Simplest; just edit the credit quotes in credit-quotes.ts + credits.ts.
  • Or raise the $/credit users pay (sell fewer credits per $) — affects all skills, simpler pricing page change.
  • Or cut cost — use Seedance 480p for drafts ($0.092/s, ~half) and only render 720p finals; or negotiate EvoLink volume rate.
  • Image skills are already comfortably above 70% — no change needed there.

Caveat: gpt-image-2 per-image cost is estimated (~$0.10); if it's higher the image margins drop but stay above 70% up to ~$0.15. Seedance + revenue numbers are exact.
